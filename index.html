CutCut

A Windows desktop app that turns long Twitch streams into vertical short videos with karaoke subtitles.

What it does

  • Downloads a Twitch stream recording or opens a video file from your computer.
  • Finds interesting moments using speech recognition, on-screen analysis, loudness and chat activity. All analysis runs locally on your computer.
  • Renders 9:16 shorts with the webcam and gameplay framed for mobile, plus word-by-word subtitles.
  • Lets you publish finished shorts to your own TikTok and YouTube accounts and track their views.

How CutCut uses TikTok

You sign in with TikTok through TikTok's own login page; CutCut never sees your password. After you sign in, CutCut can:

  • show your display name, username and avatar in the list of connected accounts (user.info.basic);
  • upload a short you chose and send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft or post it with the caption and privacy setting you picked (video.upload, video.publish);
  • read the list of your videos and their view, like, comment and share counts to show statistics for shorts published from CutCut (video.list).

CutCut never posts anything without your explicit action in the app.

How CutCut uses YouTube

You sign in with Google and choose a YouTube channel. CutCut uploads the shorts you publish to that channel and reads their statistics. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the Privacy Policy for details.

Availability

CutCut is an independent project in private testing. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, Google, YouTube or Twitch.
